UPGRADE FIRST

Install over a signed-in 1.1.0. You should stay signed in, with your Speed Dial shortcuts and Appearance settings intact.

NEW

- Activity widget: right-click the desktop > Edit Widgets… and add "Activity" (needs the Activity app on your server).
- Spotlight finds Talk conversations, notes, collectives and their pages.
- Shortcuts actions open a Talk conversation, note, collective or collective page.
- Nextcloud apps show their own icons in the View menu and in Settings… > Speed Dials.

IMPROVED AND FIXED

- Logging out removes your account from the menus, Spotlight, the widget and notifications, but keeps your Speed Dial shortcuts and Appearance settings.
- Window buttons are centered in the Nextcloud header.
- No gap in the header in full screen.
- Choosing an app that is already open brings its window forward, also Talk while it shows a conversation.
- Expired server sessions no longer sign you out.
- Email and phone links open in the app that handles them, such as Mail or FaceTime.
- Fixed a crash with live notifications.

PLEASE ALSO CHECK

- Signing in, also to an address with no server behind it.
- "Liquid Glass appearance" and "Remove gaps around content" in Settings… > Appearance.
- Showing and hiding the sidebar, dragging windows by the Nextcloud header, clicking notifications.
- Camera and microphone in Talk calls.
- Downloads (Window > Downloads).
- Servers without Talk, Notes or Collectives.
