Cirruscope brings Nextcloud to your Mac as a proper, app-like experience — not just another browser tab.

FEATURES

• No titlebar or toolbar — your Nextcloud interface fills the window edge to edge
• Drag the window by the Nextcloud top bar
• Toggle the sidebar from the app menu whenever you want more room
• Quick-launch your favorite Nextcloud server apps from the menu bar and Dock, with your own keyboard shortcuts
• Open a Nextcloud server app straight from Spotlight or a Shortcuts action — no need to open Cirruscope first
• Optional translucent window style, if you prefer it over the solid default
• Windows reopen at the same position and size after a relaunch
• Downloads land straight in your Downloads folder, with a progress window and a notification when they finish

PRIVACY

Cirruscope collects nothing. No analytics, no tracking — the only network connection it makes is to your own Nextcloud server.

FREE & OPEN SOURCE

Cirruscope is free and built entirely in the open, so you can review every line of its source code.

Cirruscope is an unofficial, independently built app from the Nextcloud community. It is not associated with or endorsed by Nextcloud GmbH.
